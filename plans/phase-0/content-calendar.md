# Phase 0 Content Calendar

## 1. 内容目标

Phase 0 的内容目标是验证市场共鸣、招募访谈对象、收集真实 query，并为 waitlist 积累第一批用户。

不要宣传产品已经成熟。重点宣传问题、观点和构建过程。

## 2. 核心观点

- Filters are the wrong interface for people search.
- Bigger lead lists are not better lead lists.
- AI people search needs evidence.
- Outreach should start from context, not templates.
- The future of outbound is high-signal research, not more automation spam.

## 4. X / Twitter 草稿

### Post 1

```text
I'm building SignalReach AI.

Instead of searching with filters, describe the person you want to find.

SignalReach searches across the web, explains why each person matches, and turns the results into personalized outreach drafts.

Starting with high-signal B2B outbound.
```

### Post 2

```text
People search is still stuck in the filter era.

Role = VP Sales
Location = US
Company size = 50-500

But that's rarely how we think.

We think:
"Find sales leaders at growing SaaS companies that are likely feeling pipeline pressure."

That's the interface I want.
```

### Post 3

```text
The problem with lead databases:

They optimize for more rows.

But outbound teams need:
- why this person
- why now
- what evidence
- how to reach out

High-signal beats high-volume.
```

### Post 4

```text
Looking for 10 people who do outbound, creator sourcing, recruiting, or expert discovery.

I want to learn how you find people today:
- tools
- workflow
- pain points
- what makes a result useful

Happy to manually help with one search in return.
```

### Post 5

```text
Early SignalReach benchmark idea:

For each people search result, score:
1. Relevance
2. Evidence quality
3. Contactability
4. Utility

If a lead has no evidence and no action path, it should not count as a good result.
```
