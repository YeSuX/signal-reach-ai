# Demo Report 02: AI Productivity Creator Sourcing

## 1. User Query

```text
Find English-speaking creators on YouTube, LinkedIn, or personal websites who focus on AI productivity, workflow automation, AI tools, or founder/operator productivity. Prioritize creators with evidence of sponsorships, business audience fit, or practical tool walkthroughs.
```

## 2. Parsed Criteria

### Must Have

- English-language content.
- Public content about AI productivity, AI tools, automation, workflow, or professional productivity.
- Audience fit for founders, operators, knowledge workers, marketers, or technical builders.
- At least one public profile, website, YouTube channel page, creator database page, or sponsorship page.

### Nice To Have

- Public sponsorship or "work with me" page.
- Evidence of practical tutorials rather than generic AI news.
- Business, founder, or operator audience.
- Public contact path such as website form, business email page, newsletter, or sponsor page.

### Exclude

- Pure AI news channels with no practical workflow content.
- Entertainment-only or faceless AI content channels.
- Creators focused only on consumer gadgets or generic tech reviews.

## 3. Search Strategy

Sources searched:

- Creator websites.
- Creator discovery databases.
- Sponsorship pages.
- YouTube-focused creator index pages.
- Reddit posts looking for AI productivity creators.

Search concepts:

- AI productivity creator sponsorship
- YouTube AI tools creator
- AI workflow automation creator
- creator productivity AI tools
- FutureTools, Skill Leap AI, AI Advantage, Jeff Su, Moe Lueker

## 5. Fit Summary

Best first outreach targets:

1. Moe Lueker.
2. Jeff Su.
3. The AI Advantage.
4. Skill Leap AI.

These four have the strongest combination of content-topic fit, professional or builder audience, and evidence that their audience cares about AI tools, practical workflows, or productivity.

Matt Wolfe is likely higher reach but may be more expensive and broader. Better Creating may be useful if the product demo can connect to productivity systems, Notion, or workspace design.

## 6. Suggested Campaign Angle

For SignalReach AI, the creator campaign should not pitch "another AI tool." The more specific angle:

```text
I used an AI agent to find high-signal people for a real outbound campaign, with evidence and personalized outreach drafts.
```

Possible video concepts:

- "I replaced manual lead research with an AI people-search agent."
- "Finding 20 high-quality startup leads using only a natural-language prompt."
- "Why better outbound starts with evidence, not bigger lead lists."

## 7. Suggested Outreach Draft

```text
Subject: Possible walkthrough for an AI people-search workflow

Hi [Name],

I’ve been following your work around AI tools and practical productivity workflows. I’m building SignalReach AI, an AI people-search agent that lets users describe who they want to find, then returns evidence-backed matches and personalized outreach drafts.

I think your audience would find the workflow interesting because it is not another generic chatbot demo: it shows how AI can compress the messy research process behind outbound, creator sourcing, or expert discovery.

Would you be open to taking a look at a short demo? Happy to share a concrete use case and let you decide whether it is useful for your audience.
```

## 8. Risks And Verification Needed

- Subscriber counts and sponsorship availability need fresh verification before outreach.
- Some creator database pages may lag behind current channel positioning.
- Creator fit should be checked against the latest 10-20 videos before paying.
- For sponsored content, confirm disclosure requirements and avoid inflated performance claims.
