## 9. FAQ

### Is this a CRM?

No. SignalReach is focused on finding and preparing high-quality people matches. CRM integrations may come later.

### Does it automatically send emails?

Not by default. Early versions generate drafts and exports. Sending requires user review and authorization.

### What data does it use?

SignalReach focuses on public, explainable sources and keeps evidence links with recommendations.

### Who is it for?

Early versions are best for founders, growth teams, sales teams, agencies, and creator marketers who frequently search for people.
